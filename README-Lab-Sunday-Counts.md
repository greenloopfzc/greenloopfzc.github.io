Sunday from 00:00 to 24:00 contributes zero time; partial Sundays are excluded by their exact duration. A return from Final QC continues the original-arrival clock.

Dashboard waiting durations and the recorded Lab-delay history use the same Sunday-free calculation. Original movement dates, arrival timestamps, assignments, stock and employee visibility are unchanged. Hidden technician cards do not remove pending phones from either total. Existing search, sort, read-only permissions, office restrictions and 50-record pagination remain in force.
