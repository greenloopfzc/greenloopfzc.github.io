How this report is counted

One line per phone job. Pass / Fail attempts stay in its history. Dates select phones by first arrival or latest result; the totals cover the full B-DEPT history of those phones. Time includes recorded periods inside B-DEPT and stops when the phone passes or leaves. Time outside B-DEPT is excluded. Missing arrival records show “Time not recorded”. Average time includes passed phones with recorded times. All dates use UAE (UTC+4).
