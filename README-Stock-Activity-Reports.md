# Stock Production, Export and Received Reports

Ready Stock's Stock Activity Reports section has three matching cards: Stock Production Report, Stock Export Report and Stock Received Report. Each card has Today / Monthly on its first line and Average Daily / Average Monthly on its second line. Each figure is separately clickable. Dates and denominators are visible beside the figures; the selected activity date controls both the day and month-to-date scope.

Today counts preserve the previous Ready arrivals, confirmed exports and original receiving quantities exactly. Production/export retain the existing inclusive10am–9pm UAE window, one phone per day. A phone processed again on another day contributes to that day's production/export; month totals sum the daily entries, not unique original receipt units. Current physical Ready Stock, original stock quantities and inventory balances remain unchanged. Receiving uses the full UAE day and original receipt quantity, including unentered stock; internal replacement allocation receipts are excluded.

Monthly means the calendar month's first day through the selected date. Daily average keeps the previous30 calendar-day window, excluding Sundays and the selected date; zero-activity working days remain in the25/26-day denominator. Monthly average uses up to3 completed calendar months before the selected month. Available recorded history begins with each metric's first calendar month containing a qualifying positive activity; months before that recorded history are not invented as zero. Zero-activity months within the available range are included. The current partial month is excluded. The exact month range and number of months are displayed per card. With no completed recorded month, the average is unavailable (an em dash), not a fabricated zero.

Daily/monthly averages open their exact day/month tables, including zero periods, with a link to the selected period's records. Production/export monthly and historical-period records retain Show Color, Model Summary, GB x Grade matrix and exact IMEIs,50 records per page. Model/grade quantities and clicked period totals are verified against the same scope. Receiving opens original stock receipts,50 per page. No full detail pages are automatically fetched. Current-day access retains View; historical/month/average drilldowns and date selection require the existing Search grant. Supplier names retain their independent permission. Operator page, role, office and TV restrictions are enforced server-side.

SQL323 adds read-only APIs and private helpers. It reads the bounded activity range once per context and builds exact daily/monthly totals and review snapshots. Index-backed first-history queries avoid running the full daily reader for every historical date. Monthly grouping/model workers reuse the reviewed context payload and the existing grouping/matrix behavior. No earlier routine is overwritten, no historical events are repaired/backfilled and no inventory quantities are written. Changing membership, device metadata or the permitted name view invalidates stale review snapshots. Explicit list/model Refresh advances the reviewed scope; background summary updates never silently alter an open model's snapshot. Late office/access replies are ignored and private dialogs clear on lock/sign-out.
