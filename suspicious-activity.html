Monitoring coverage & context

Alerts highlight records that need a human review. They do not establish misconduct and never deduct pay or block a phone automatically.

Successful login browser/device type, configured working hours, Ready Stock workflow, IMEI corrections, damage corrections and verified box corrections are monitored. First login establishes a baseline. Failed passwords, exact device identity and location are not monitored by this release.

Only new activity after installation is monitored. Historical imports need a separately designed import workflow so old transactions are not treated as live operations.
