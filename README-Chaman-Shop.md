# Chaman Shop

Greenloop Section has three main cards: Transfer Stock Entry, Returned Stock Entry, Reports. Chaman Shop Section has Received Stock Entry, Returned Stock Entry, Reports. Reports opens two nested cards: Greenloop = Transferred Stock Data / Received Stock Data; Chaman = Received Stock Data / Returned Stock Data. Stock Balance Data is temporarily removed from both sections; its data/APIs and inventory calculations remain available. No table loads on the landing screen. Admin can switch sections; each still shows only its three main cards.

Any allowed non-shop account defaults to Greenloop. User Access has one special flag, This is the Chaman Shop login. View, Entry, Search and PDF/Excel are separate grants; existing staff gains no automatic page access. Every RPC enforces active accounts, office access and the permitted side independently from the browser. Owner / Super Admin can operate both sections. No sales/customer-account process is included.

Entry pages use compact rows. A15-digit scan loads model, GB, color, GL Grade, original supplier/batch quantity and stage at dispatch, then focuses the next row. Fast scans queue while details load; explicit bulk paste retains all lines. Preview is read-only. Save / Confirm checks a reviewed snapshot and retry key before stock moves. Receiving scans can span multiple deliveries and are matched to their exact pending documents in one transaction. Partial receiving remains explicit. Reports have date-wise documents, whole red pending rows and whole green received rows, 50-row UI pages, and complete PDF/Excel downloads independent of pagination. Dates have no time and no device-number columns are added.

Stock dispatch subtracts company availability immediately on confirmation. Chaman receipt confirms physical shop balance. Chaman return dispatch keeps the phones outside company stock; only Greenloop confirmed receipt restores each phone's original saved workflow stage. Original phone/job, supplier, receipt quantities and batches stay preserved. Active work, other active jobs and export-box reservations block dispatch; pending Initial QC, Parts, Lab & Glass, B-DEPT and Final QC stages are supported. A phone cannot be sent twice or inserted into another company job/export box while outside Greenloop.

Original received stock remains visible. For500 received phones, dispatch60+40 gives company400; return dispatch50 leaves company400; Greenloop receipt20 then30 gives company450 + Chaman50. Original received remains500. Accounts Supplier Stock and IMEI DATA show scoped Chaman movement summaries. IMEI DATA and its PDF/Excel include Stage / Location. Supplier Progress shows sent, received-back, physical/transit and pending quantities without duplicating unentered receipt-balance rows. Customer Stock retains its existing scope.

Private append-only Chaman events preserve dispatch, Chaman receipt, return dispatch, Greenloop receipt, receipt undo and Admin corrections. Existing trip/correction snapshots are backfilled; repeating installation does not duplicate live events. IMEI Search includes complete dated events, current custody and transfer/return totals, while keeping existing receiving, QC, parts, supplier return/replacement and other journey records.

Reports > Chaman Transfer Correction is Admin-only: edit transfer date/notes, undo receiving, remove selected mistaken IMEIs or delete a transfer. A reason, current reviewed snapshot and stable retry key are required. Private before/after audit preserves deleted IMEI/model/batch data; Deleted History links to those transfers. Outbound removal restores company stock; return removal keeps the phone at Chaman. Reversing completed receiving checks original job/device/batch/status, later trips, other active jobs and packing reservations. Immutable custody ordering prevents a corrected display date bypassing later-trip protection. Phones/jobs and transfer audit are not hard-deleted.
